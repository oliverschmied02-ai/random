Welcome to Your Personal Finance Dashboard

This app helps you manage your household finances by:

  • 📤 Uploading N26 bank transactions (CSV)
  • 🏷️ Auto-categorizing transactions (with manual override)
  • 📊 Populating your financial statements in Google Sheets
  • 💳 Tracking loan repayment schedules
  • 📈 Viewing your net worth and P&L
First Time Setup:
  1. Click "Connect Google Sheets" below
  2. Create a new Google Sheet (or use existing one)
  3. Return here and upload your N26 CSV
🔐 Connect Google Sheets

How It Works

Step 1: Upload CSV

Export your N26 transactions as CSV and upload them.

Step 2: Review & Categorize

The system suggests categories based on your previous transactions. You can override them.

Step 3: Auto-Sync to Google Sheets

Your Google Sheet automatically updates with the categorized transactions. Your formulas for P&L, balance sheet, etc. update instantly.
